## What "not vibecoded" means here (checklist every agent applies)

- **No:** a gradient on every card, emoji as icons, a glow or glass on everything, centered marketing copy inside the app, 3-column cards of equal weight for content that isn't equal, "✨ AI-powered" labels, lorem-style filler, random border radii, shadows plus borders plus rings together, more than one accent colour competing, every element animating on load, or long wrapping paragraphs of explanation on working screens.
- **Yes:**
  - One clear primary action per screen, and real hierarchy (one big thing, then supporting things).
  - A consistent 4/8px spacing scale, aligned grid edges, and tabular numerals.
  - Icons from one set at one stroke width: lucide-style SVGs drawn inline, 1.5px stroke, 16 or 20px.
  - Copy that is short and specific.
  - Empty states that tell you the next action.
  - Skeletons that match the final layout.
  - Focus states, and keyboard paths (⌘K, Esc, arrow keys in menus).
- **Density:** a working screen shows **at most 3 primary regions** above the fold. Move explanations into a help popover, a "?" tooltip or the `/learn` course, not inline paragraphs. Ledes are one line (≤ 90 characters) or none.

## Motion system (one place, used everywhere)

- Put shared motion in `src/components/motion/` and `globals.css` tokens: `--dur-1: 120ms`, `--dur-2: 200ms`, `--dur-3: 320ms`, `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, `--ease-inout: cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`, and spring 380/32 for pills.
- Motion that is allowed:
  - Staggered entrance of a page's regions (40–60ms apart, translateY 6px plus fade).
  - The sliding pill in segmented controls and the rail.
  - Count-ups on stat numbers.
  - Sparklines that draw in.
  - Hover lift on cards that are links.
  - Number changes that roll.
  - The theme wipe.
  - Folder hover and open on the volunteer side.
  - Route transitions via the View Transitions API where supported.
  - Toasts that slide in from the bottom.
- The check-in, event-day and verify paths never wait on animation (see DESIGN-BRIEF round 2).
- Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (no movement, no wipe, instant swap). Content is visible at rest: never gate visibility on JS animation finishing. The landing hero currently paints almost blank at first, so fix that pattern.

## Coordinator side (`/org`)

- **Shell:** the Vessa top bar and icon rail described above, replacing the current sidebar and nav. The rail holds the sections: Home, Events, Volunteers, Messages, Reports, plus the rarely used items in a "More" menu (Badges, Learn, Settings). On phones, the rail becomes a bottom tab bar of 4 items plus More. ⌘K opens a command palette (jump to an event, a volunteer or a page; "New event"). "+ New" opens a small menu (Event, Broadcast, Import paper sheet).
- **Home:**
  - The welcome card says what needs you now, e.g. "1 event waiting to be verified", with that button.
  - Three stat cards with sparklines: verified hours (30 days), unique volunteers, and events verified within 24h.
  - A "Happening now / next" card with the live event, its code and the headcount.
  - One chart (hours by event) with a hover tooltip.
  - That's it. Everything else moves to its own page.
- **Every list page** (events, volunteers, messages, reports): an eyebrow and title, one range or filter segmented control, one primary action, then the content. No stacked explanation paragraphs.

## Volunteer side (`/me`, `/discover`, `/checkin`, `/join`, `/v`, `/w`)

- Mobile-first app shell: a top bar with the greeting and avatar, and a **bottom tab bar** (Home, Discover, Messages, Me). Big thumb targets.
- **Home:**
  - A hero "your verified hours" number (count-up) with "+N this month".
  - An active session card if one is checked in (live timer, one Check out button).
  - "Up next": the next event signed up for.
  - **Your organizations as folders:** each org a folder card with its colour tile, total hours and record count. Hovering (or long-press on touch) lifts the papers. Tapping opens the folder: record cards fan out, and each opens the record.
- **Record page:** the record wording as a clean certificate-style card, with Share (link, QR, copy) and "Use this record" (the blurb) as two clear actions. The minor rule stays (no share without a guardian OK).
- **Pending hours:** show "Waiting for {org} to confirm" with no number.
- **Account screen (new or reworked):** name, email, theme toggle, message opt-in (marketing consent, off by default), **Download my data** (JSON of the volunteer's own rows), **Delete my account** (a confirm step; deletes the volunteer and unlinks their attendance, keeping the org's attested totals as anonymous rows; explain this in one line), and links to Privacy and Terms.
- **Join:** a required "I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy" checkbox (for 13–17: "and a parent or guardian knows I'm using this"). Keep the under-13 refusal.
